Featured project · open source

Plaid

A home streaming service in one sitting. Answer four questions in your browser or your terminal: Plaid installs seven open-source apps, links them together and hands you the address of each one.

  • Seven apps, wired upJellyfin, Seerr, Radarr, Sonarr, qBittorrent, Prowlarr and Bazarr talk to each other through their APIs from the first minute.
  • Removed without a traceOne click takes everything back out: containers, volumes, settings.
  • Looks after itselfDisk and VPN guards, one-click updates, language and quality profiles, backups when you ask.
  • Runs anywhereLinux, Synology, Raspberry Pi, Mac and Windows, with optional VPN and Tailscale.
Visit the site Source Latest release

Python (stdlib only), Docker Compose, vanilla JS
